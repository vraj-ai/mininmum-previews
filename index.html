Three directions for the mininmum shop

Each one is a working demo. Browse the sets, open a product, choose a size, add to the demo bag and change quantities. All three use the same four prints, so the comparison is about design and shopping, not products.

Sample products, photos and details for review. No prices are set and nothing can be bought.

Story Shelf

Every print is a bedtime story. Shopping feels like choosing tonight's book.

  • Composition: a centred picture-book column with the print as endpaper
  • Type: Young Serif with Literata for reading
  • Interaction: read a story line by line, switch stories by their spines

Measured

Clarity builds trust. Every fact a mother needs, shown precisely and calmly.

  • Composition: a left-aligned spec sheet with square, consistent photos
  • Type: Schibsted Grotesk with IBM Plex Mono for measurements
  • Interaction: a garment drawing that redraws its lengths as you pick a size

Little Rooms

See the sets where they live. Tap what you like and shop it without leaving the photo.

  • Composition: photo-first and app-like, with rounded frames and a mosaic
  • Type: Zen Maru Gothic, echoing the rounded logo
  • Interaction: shoppable photos that open a quick-add sheet
